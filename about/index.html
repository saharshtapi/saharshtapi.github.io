Saharsh Agrawal

Security Researcher · Threat Intelligence · Pentester

Hi there! I'm a security researcher who spends most of my time finding bugs in web applications, taking apart malware campaigns, and writing up exactly how the attack worked. I care about the part after the exploit: the detection, the write-up, the tooling that makes the next hunt faster.

Everything published on this site is my own work — CVE analysis, CTF walkthroughs, threat research and the tools I build along the way. When I'm not in front of a terminal you'll find me out on the racetrack.

Vulnerability Research

Finding and reporting real vulnerabilities in production web applications — from open redirects and access-control flaws to injection issues — and writing them up with a reproducible proof of concept.

Penetration Testing

Full-scope offensive engagements against infrastructure and applications, from initial reconnaissance through to privilege escalation and lateral movement.

Threat Intelligence

Tracking how commodity malware is delivered and updated — PaperCut exploitation, IcedID, Qakbot, OneNote campaigns and Aurora Stealer — and turning that research into detections teams can actually deploy.

Automation & Tooling

Building the small tools that remove the repetitive parts of a hunt: subdomain enumeration wrappers, data pipelines and analysis scripts that make the work repeatable.

    Created Sub3num, a subdomain-enumeration wrapper that chains Sublist3r, Assetfinder, Subfinder, Findomain, Amass, httprobe and subjack into one pass. Continued with the Overpass, ColddBox and Advent of Cyber 3 writeups.

    Built a Python, Pandas and NumPy pipeline that pulls and analyses NEPSE floor sheet data, turning a raw daily dump into something you can actually read and trade off.
