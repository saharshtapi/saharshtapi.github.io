Vulnerability Research

Source review and dynamic testing of web applications to find real, reportable bugs — open redirects, broken access control and injection flaws — then writing them up as CVEs.

Penetration Testing

End-to-end offensive engagements: enumeration, exploitation, privilege escalation and lateral movement, with every step captured so the fix is obvious at the end.

Threat Intelligence

Malware and intrusion analysis published with the research team at Loginsoft — tracking how commodity loaders such as IcedID, Qakbot and Aurora are delivered and evolved.

Automation & Tooling

Small, focused tools that remove the repetitive parts of recon and analysis, plus data pipelines that turn raw market and telemetry data into something you can act on.
